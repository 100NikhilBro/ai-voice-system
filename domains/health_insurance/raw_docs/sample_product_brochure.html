Plan Tiers Overview

HealthShield Silver Plan

Designed for basic individual protection with essential inpatient hospital coverage.

  • Sum Insured Options: $50,000 or $100,000 annually.
  • Hospital Room Rent Limit: Capped at 1% of Sum Insured per day ($150/day max).
  • Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Limit: Capped at 2% of Sum Insured per day.
  • Outpatient Consultation (OPD): Not included in Silver tier.
  • Pharmacy Copay: 20% mandatory copayment on prescription medications.

HealthShield Gold Plan (Most Popular)

Comprehensive protection for individuals and growing families with enhanced room benefits.

  • Sum Insured Options: $200,000 or $350,000 annually.
  • Hospital Room Rent Limit: Single Private Room covered without daily dollar cap.
  • ICU Charges: 100% covered up to annual sum insured.
  • Outpatient Consultation (OPD): Up to $1,000 per policy year (annual deductible $50).
  • Cashless Network: Access to over 4,500 in-network partner hospitals nationwide.
  • Ambulance Service: Covered up to $500 per emergency event.

HealthShield Platinum Plan

Executive-level comprehensive healthcare coverage with zero room rent sub-limits and worldwide emergency coverage.

  • Sum Insured Options: $500,000 or $1,000,000 annually.
  • Hospital Room Rent Limit: Deluxe Suite covered with zero sub-limits.
  • Outpatient Consultation (OPD): Up to $2,500 per policy year with $0 deductible.
  • Alternative Medicine Rider: Acupuncture and chiropractic care covered up to $750/yr.
  • Annual Health Checkup: Complimentary comprehensive biometric screening for all adult insured members.
