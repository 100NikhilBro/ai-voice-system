### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### FAQ 1: How does the cashless hospitalization admission process work at network hospitals?
- **Planned Hospitalization**: For planned surgeries or admissions, present your HealthShield digital health card at the hospital's Third-Party Administrator (TPA) helpdesk at least **48 hours prior to admission**. The TPA sends an electronic pre-authorization request to HealthShield, which is processed within 2 hours.
- **Emergency Hospitalization**: For emergency treatments, present your card within **24 hours of hospital admission**. The hospital will initiate emergency pre-authorization to guarantee cashless admission.
- **Settlement**: At discharge, HealthShield settles approved medical expenses directly with the network hospital. The policyholder only pays non-medical expenses (e.g., telephone charges, visitor meals, or specific copayments).

#### FAQ 2: What is the claim reimbursement procedure if I receive treatment at an out-of-network hospital?
- If treatment is received at a licensed hospital outside our 4,500+ cashless network:
  1. Pay the hospital bills directly upon discharge.
  2. Obtain itemized hospital bills, payment receipts, diagnostic lab reports, and the physician's signed discharge summary.
  3. Submit the claim via our digital portal or mail within **30 calendar days of discharge**.
  4. Approved reimbursement payments are remitted via direct bank deposit within **7 to 10 business days** of receiving complete documentation.

### Grounded Objection Handling Scripts

#### Objection 1: "Why is the waiting period for pre-existing conditions so long (2 to 3 years)? Other cheap policies advertise immediate coverage!"
- **Grounded Response Rationale**:
  - Explain the fundamental insurance pooling principle: Statutory waiting periods (24 months for Platinum, 36 months for Gold) protect the collective risk pool against adverse selection—preventing people from buying policies solely when already ill, which causes premiums to spike for everyone.
  - Highlight immediate protection: Accidental bodily emergencies and acute injuries are covered from **Day 1**. General new illnesses are covered after just **30 days**.
  - Caution regarding deceptive policies: Low-cost policies advertising "immediate coverage" often hide permanent exclusions or 50% copayment clauses in fine print. HealthShield offers guaranteed comprehensive coverage once the statutory waiting period elapses.

#### Objection 2: "Why do health insurance premiums increase as I get older, even if I haven't filed any claims?"
- **Grounded Response Rationale**:
  - Premiums are calculated on actuarial age-band cohorts (5-year bands, e.g., 30–34, 35–39). As biological age increases, the statistical frequency of hospitalizations and medical inflation naturally rises across the entire population pool.
  - To reward loyal and healthy policyholders, HealthShield provides a **Cumulative No-Claim Bonus (NCB)**: For every consecutive claim-free year, your Sum Insured automatically increases by 10% (up to a maximum 50% bonus) with zero increase in base premium.

#### Objection 3: "Is pre-existing diabetes or hypertension automatically rejected during qualification?"
- **Grounded Response Rationale**:
  - No, declared diabetes (Type-2) or hypertension does not result in automatic rejection.
  - Under HealthShield Gold and Platinum underwriting rules, applicants with stable, medically managed hypertension or diabetes are routinely approved, provided they are declared transparently and comply with the continuous coverage waiting period (24 months on Platinum, 36 months on Gold).
