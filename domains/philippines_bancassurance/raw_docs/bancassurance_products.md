# HealthShield Bancassurance Life Protection Guide (Philippines)
## Confidential Internal Underwriting & Product Guidelines v2.4

### 1. Product Overview: HealthShield Bancassurance Life Plans
HealthShield Philippines partners with premier commercial banks to offer integrated life insurance and savings solutions directly through branch referrals.

#### Available Plan Tiers:
- **Bancassurance Secure Life (Term Life Tier)**:
  - Entry Age: 18 to 60 years old (renewable up to age 75).
  - Minimum Annual Premium: ₱18,000 / year (or ₱1,650 / month via auto-debit).
  - Coverage: ₱1,000,000 guaranteed death benefit.
  - Included Riders: Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D) Rider (additional ₱500,000).

- **Bancassurance Prime Future (Endowment & Investment Tier)**:
  - Entry Age: 20 to 55 years old.
  - Minimum Annual Premium: ₱36,000 / year (or ₱3,200 / month).
  - Coverage: Guaranteed life coverage plus guaranteed cash payouts starting at year 5.
  - Included Riders: Waiver of Premium upon Total & Permanent Disability (TPD).

- **Bancassurance Critical Shield (Critical Illness Tier)**:
  - Entry Age: 18 to 50 years old.
  - Covers 45 major critical illnesses (cancer, stroke, coronary artery bypass).
  - Lump-sum benefit: ₱1,500,000 upon verified diagnosis.

---

### 2. Underwriting & Qualification Rules
- **Residency**: Must be a legal resident of the Republic of the Philippines.
- **Branch Referral Verification**: Inquire which partner bank branch referred the customer (e.g., BDO, BPI, Metrobank branch).
- **Payment Modes**: Annual, semi-annual, quarterly, or monthly auto-debit (*hulog*).
- **Grace Period**: 31 calendar days from the scheduled premium due date.
- **Policy Lapse Rule**: If payment is not received within the 31-day grace period, the policy will lapse. Reinstatement is permitted within 2 years subject to health insurability evidence.
